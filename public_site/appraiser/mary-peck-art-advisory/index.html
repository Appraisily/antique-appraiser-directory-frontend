Mary Peck Art Advisory

Denver, Colorado modern, contemporary, emerging, Impressionist, and Modern art appraisal and advisory firm.

Mary Peck Art Advisory, LLC was founded in 2008 after Mary Peck's work at Christie's New York, where public sources state she was a Specialist and Vice President in Post-War and Contemporary Art. The official site and Appraisers Association profile support appraisal work for collectors, foundations, corporate collections, institutions, and art investment funds.
