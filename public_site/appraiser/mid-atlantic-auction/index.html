Mid-Atlantic Auction & Appraisals

Mid-Atlantic Auction & Appraisals is a source-backed antique appraisal and auction business founded by Edward Stinson. Public source pages describe written formal antique appraisals for estates and single items, verbal appraisals for items someone may sell, estate-sale and auction support, and buying support for quality antiques.
