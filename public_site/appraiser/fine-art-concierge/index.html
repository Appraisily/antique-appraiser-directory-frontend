Fine Art Concierge

Philadelphia, PA

Fine Art Concierge is a Philadelphia fine art appraisal and advisory firm led by Cliff Bailer. Official source pages describe help for people downsizing an art collection or settling an estate, with guidance through auctions, galleries, art fairs, private dealers, internet art markets, valuation research, and sale planning.

Specialties

  • •Art appraisal and valuation advice for estates, trusts, heirs, executors, collectors, wealth managers, insurance professionals, and art owners
  • •Post War and Contemporary, Modern and Impressionist, Old Master paintings and prints, folk art, Americana, and 19th-century European and American art
  • •Philadelphia art, New Hope School, Barnes Foundation artists, early 20th-century modernism, Tiffany decorative arts, Wyeth circle, illustrators, African American art, and mid-century modern
  • •NYU appraisal certificate and Appraiser Qualification Board USPAP certification listed by the official about page

Services

  • •Free introductory phone consultation
  • •Art collection downsizing and estate-settlement guidance
  • •Research to establish authenticity, artist context, and market hierarchy
  • •Guidance comparing auction houses, private dealers, galleries, art fairs, and online art-market options
