Gemological Appraisal Laboratory of America, Inc. (GemLab)

580 Fifth Avenue, Suite 2906, New York, NY 10036

GemLab is a New York Diamond District appraisal laboratory for jewelry, diamonds, gems, pearls, and watches. The official site describes appraisals and lab reports for insurance, trade, estate, tax, legal, and personal purposes, with NYC diamond screening available at the 580 Fifth Avenue location.
