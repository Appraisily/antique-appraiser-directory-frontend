Mussallem Galleries

Jacksonville, Florida

Mussallem Galleries is an established Jacksonville fine art and antiques gallery. The official site says the gallery was established in 1897 and lists European paintings, Oriental rugs, antique silver, objets d'art, Oriental antiques, American art, contemporary art, and antique furniture among its featured categories.

Free Verbal Appraisal Workflow

Mussallem Galleries' official appraisal page invites visitors to submit photos and information about antiques, Oriental rugs, silver, paintings, estate items, and related objects. The page says the gallery will respond with a free verbal appraisal and indicate whether it would like to purchase the item.
