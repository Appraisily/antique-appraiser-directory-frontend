Montclair Estates

Oakland, CA

Montclair Estates is an Oakland antiques, consignment, and appraisal business operated by Niels Dahl-Jensen. The official site describes ISA Accredited Member credentials, USPAP-compliant personal property appraisals, and more than 30 years of valuation and sale experience for antiques, jewelry, collectibles, fine art, decorative arts, and other personal property.
