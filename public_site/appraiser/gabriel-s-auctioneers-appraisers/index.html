Gabriel's Auctioneers & Appraisers

Norwood and Boston, MA

Gabriel's Auctioneers & Appraisers is a full-service auction and consignment company serving New England from Norwood, MA. Official materials describe operation since 1974, free phone initial consultation, appraisal services, on-site estate and moving sales, outright purchases, estate cleanouts, internet auctions, business liquidations, real estate services, real estate auctions and foreclosures, and fundraising auctions.

Specialties

  • -Estate and inheritance appraisals, market value appraisals, insurance appraisal reports, antique and estate auctions, consignments, and outright purchases
  • -American and European antiques, Victorian and Continental furniture, Arts and Crafts, Art Deco, Art Nouveau, Tiffany and Steuben glass, porcelain, pottery, clocks, watches, paintings, prints, drawings, bronzes, rugs, sterling silver, jewelry, dolls, toys, trains, firearms, military, nautical items, musical instruments, textiles, and more
  • -Evan Gavrilles, chief auctioneer and appraiser since 1974, with official team materials noting past Massachusetts State Auctioneers Association presidency and CAI training

Services

  • -Verbal or written formal appraisal documents for estate and inheritance, equitable distribution, fair market value, insurance, and other valuations
  • -Estate tag sales, estate auctions, online auction platforms, consignment, outright purchase, charitable donation distribution, and complete broom-sweep cleanout services
  • -Business liquidations, machinery and equipment auctions, office furnishings, store fixtures, inventories, restaurant and construction assets, real estate auctions, and foreclosure auctions
