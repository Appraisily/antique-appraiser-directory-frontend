Appraisals By The Sea, Inc.

Delray Beach, FL

Appraisals by the Sea Inc. is a Delray Beach personal property appraisal firm led by Marie Wayland, an Accredited Appraiser of the International Society of Appraisers. The firm serves South Florida and traveling client needs with appraisal support for household contents, fine art, antiques, jewelry, estates, divorce, insurance, donations, damage claims, realty-related transactions, and downsizing.

Services
