Dudley & Dudley Certified Personal Property Appraisers

Albany and Glens Falls, NY

Dudley & Dudley presents itself as a board certified personal property appraisal firm serving Albany and Glens Falls, New York. The official site says the firm appraises fine art, antiques, textiles including historic dress and high end couture, historical documents, books, estates, estate consulting, residential contents, and institutional collections.

Specialties

  • •Certified personal property appraisals
  • •Fine art, antiques, residential contents, and period American furniture
  • •Textiles, historic dress, high end couture, historical documents, and books
  • •Estate valuation, estate consulting, collections management, and expert testimony

Services

  • •Internal Revenue Service appraisal services
  • •Estate valuation, estate planning, non-cash charitable donation, and gift tax appraisal work
  • •Equitable distribution, divorce, liquidation insurance, damage, and loss appraisal work
  • •Individual, estate, corporate, institutional, and general household collection appraisals
  • •Consultation, brokerage, collections management, and legal expert testimony

Credentials and Appraisal Standard

The official site identifies Patricia Dudley as A.A.A. and U.S.P.A.P., says she maintains a current USPAP certificate, and describes Dudley & Dudley as certified personal property appraisers. It also states that Dudley & Dudley have taken USPAP training and passed the required examinations to create USPAP-compliant appraisals.
