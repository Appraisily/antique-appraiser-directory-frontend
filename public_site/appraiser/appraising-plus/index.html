Appraising Plus

Durham, NC

Appraising Plus is a Durham, NC personal property appraisal firm owned by John and Virginia Kline. The firm provides estate, insurance, divorce, equitable distribution, whole-house inventory, consultation, online selling, and auction-referral support for tangible personal property and special collections.
