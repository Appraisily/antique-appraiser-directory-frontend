La Jolla Gem Appraisal

San Diego jewelry, diamond, gemstone and watch appraisal laboratory.

Source-Backed Profile

La Jolla Gem Appraisal is an independent jewelry appraisal and consulting practice serving San Diego and nearby communities including La Jolla, Del Mar, Rancho Santa Fe and Coronado. The official site identifies Trang Pham as a GIA Graduate Gemologist, ASA Accredited Senior Appraiser and Master Gemologist Appraiser.

Public La Jolla Gem Appraisal pages describe AGA Certified Gemological Laboratory services, USPAP and ASA standards, jewelry appraisal reports, diamond and gemstone analysis, insurance appraisals, estate settlement appraisals, resale appraisals, expert witness work, and jewelry brokering.
