Lennie Rose Estate Appraisals, Inc.

Serving NYC, the tri-state area, Chicago, and the Midwest

Lennie Rose Estate Appraisals, Inc. provides personal property and estate appraisal services for New York clients and a broader service area that includes NYC, the tri-state area, Chicago, and the Midwest. Public source material says the firm works with individuals, families, executors, heirs, trust and estate attorneys, financial planners, insurance clients, and residential realtors.

Specialties

  • •Personal property, estate, antique, furniture, and art appraisals
  • •Furnishings, antiques, decorative arts, estate contents, collectibles, fine or costume jewelry, silver, and vintage clothing
  • •Estate tax, insurance, donation, estate planning, and documentation appraisals
  • •Written, verbal, virtual, onsite, and hybrid appraisal assignments

Services

  • •USPAP-compliant written appraisal reports for insurance, taxation, non-cash charitable donation, estate planning, and documentation
  • •Insurance scheduling and damage/loss claim appraisal reports
  • •Estate-content valuation and dispersal guidance for heirs, executors, attorneys, and families
  • •Verbal and virtual market value appraisal options when a formal written report is not required
