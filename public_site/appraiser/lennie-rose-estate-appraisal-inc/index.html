Lennie Rose Estate Appraisals, Inc.

Serving NYC, the tri-state area, Chicago, and the Midwest

Lennie Rose Estate Appraisals, Inc. provides personal property and estate appraisal services for New York clients and a broader service area that includes NYC, the tri-state area, Chicago, and the Midwest. Public source material says the firm works with individuals, families, executors, heirs, trust and estate attorneys, financial planners, insurance clients, and residential realtors.
