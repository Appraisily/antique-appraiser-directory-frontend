New York Fine Art Appraisers

New York, NY

New York Fine Art Appraisers is an independent fine and decorative art appraisal firm with a New York office at 445 Park Ave. Official NYFAA materials describe appraisal, advisory, authentication, and collection-management work for private collectors, institutions, insurers, attorneys, fiduciaries, and government agencies.

Specialties

  • •Fine and decorative art
  • •Paintings, drawings, and sculpture
  • •Furniture, decorations, jewelry, and personal property

Services

  • •Estate tax and estate planning appraisals
  • •Insurance coverage, damage, and loss appraisals
  • •Donation and equitable distribution appraisals
  • •Sale advisory, financial planning, authentication, and collection management support
