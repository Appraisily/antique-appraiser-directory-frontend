Heritage Auctions

Dallas, TX

Heritage Auctions publishes formal appraisal services for tangible property through an Appraisal Services Department and a specialist network covering art and collectible fields. The official appraisal page says Heritage provides Fair Market Value and Replacement Cost appraisals and free confidential auction estimate valuations for clients considering auction sale.

Specialties

  • •Fine art, antiques, collectibles, and tangible property appraisal support
  • •Fair Market Value and Replacement Cost appraisals
  • •Free auction estimate valuations for auction-worthy property

Services

  • •Formal appraisals for estate planning, estate tax, insurance, charitable donation, trusts, divorce, equitable distribution, bankruptcy, and expert witness needs
  • •Appraisals that Heritage says meet required IRS, legal, and USPAP conditions for qualified valuations of tangible property
  • •Auction proposals and auction estimate valuations for eligible property

Pricing

Formal appraisal fees are published as hourly or daily specialist rates plus applicable travel and out-of-pocket expenses, with estimated fees agreed before appraisal work begins. Heritage also publishes free and confidential auction estimate valuations for clients considering selling through Heritage.
