Grogan & Company

20 Charles Street, Boston, MA 02114

Grogan & Company is a Boston Beacon Hill auction house founded in 1987 that provides formal written appraisal services for estate tax, estate planning, family division, and insurance needs. The firm's appraisal page states that its appraisals are USPAP-compliant and accepted by the IRS.
