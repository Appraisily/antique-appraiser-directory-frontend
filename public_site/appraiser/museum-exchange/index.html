Museum Exchange

Online art appraisal coordination based in New York

Museum Exchange is an online platform for art donations and appraisal coordination. Its public appraisal page says clients can submit artwork information online and be assigned an art appraiser from Museum Exchange's network to complete a qualified appraisal.

Primary Keyword Intent

Online art appraisal services.

Appraisal Use Cases

  • •Charitable donation appraisals for art gifts to museums, hospitals, universities, and other nonprofit organizations
  • •Insurance-purpose art appraisals and updated collection valuations
  • •Tax and estate planning appraisals, including estate and inheritance contexts
  • •Art lending, asset management, and financial planning valuation needs

Online Appraisal Process

  1. Identify the artwork to be appraised.
  2. Provide the appraisal purpose, photos, artist, title, year, medium, dimensions, condition, and provenance.
  3. Submit the request online for Museum Exchange review and appraiser assignment.
  4. Receive a qualified appraisal digitally; Museum Exchange describes a typical timeline of about two weeks after complete submission.

Art Appraiser Network

Museum Exchange's public appraisal page describes a network of third-party qualified art appraisers with Appraisers Association of America Certified Member credentials, USPAP compliance, and experience with works across media and periods valued from $5,000 to over $1 million.
