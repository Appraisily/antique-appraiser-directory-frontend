Artrium Appraisal & Advisory

Cedar Rapids, IA

Artrium Appraisal & Advisory provides IRS and USPAP-compliant appraisal reports, advisory services, collection management, and litigation support for fine art and other personal property assets across the Midwest and beyond. The firm is led by Elizabeth Krambeer, an IRS Qualified Appraiser and Accredited Member of the International Society of Appraisers.

  • •Fine art from Old Masters to Contemporary works, including American, European, Asian, Aboriginal, Native American, and Russian art
  • •Decorative art, antiques, furniture, carpets, sterling silver, lighting, art glass, porcelain, jewelry, watches, books, manuscripts, collectibles, artifacts, and specialized property
  • •Large collection management, advisory, authentication support, expert witness, and litigation support

Services

  • •Estate tax, gift tax, charitable donation, insurance, equitable distribution, collateral and lending, damage or loss, and expert witness appraisals
  • •Appraisal reviews, inventory reports, collection management, estate planning support, and advisory services
  • •Consultation, property documentation, market research, USPAP-compliant reporting, and digital appraisal services where appropriate
