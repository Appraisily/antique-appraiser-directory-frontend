Edgewood Coin

Jacksonville, FL

Edgewood Coin is a Jacksonville rare coin, paper currency, and precious-metals shop established in 1963. The official site says Edgewood Coin buys, sells, and appraises rare coins, paper money, bullion, and related tangible investments across Jacksonville, Northeast Florida, and Southeast Georgia.

Limited directory listing. Confirm provider details on the official website before engagement.

Specialties

  • •Rare U.S. coins, world coins, ancient coins, and artifacts
  • •Paper money, collectible currency, Confederate currency, obsolete notes, military payment certificates, and historic banknotes
  • •Gold and silver bullion, platinum, palladium, sterling silverware, and pre-1964 90% silver
  • •PCGS, NGC, PMG, certified coins and currency, collector supplies, and numismatic education

Services

  • •Free verbal appraisals on coin collections and bullion
  • •Paid written appraisals, generally for insurance or estate purposes
  • •Buying and selling rare coins, paper money, precious metals, and coin collecting supplies
  • •Collection drop-offs for purchase evaluation or appraisal

Scope Note

The official about page says Edgewood Coin does not offer official appraisals on jewelry, does not deal in most antiques or stamps, and generally needs to see coins in person for evaluations because price depends on date of issue, mintage, condition or grade, popularity, demand, and liquidity.
