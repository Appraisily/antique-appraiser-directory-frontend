Devine & Co. Private Jeweler & Appraiser

Monterey Peninsula certified jewelry appraisal services

Devine & Co. is a private jeweler and certified appraiser serving Carmel, Monterey, and surrounding areas with jewelry appraisal, gemological evaluation, custom design, heirloom repurposing, and expert consulting services.

Source-Backed Appraisal Services

  • •Replacement value appraisals for insurance purposes
  • •Fair market value appraisals for divorce, donation, estate, probate, and bankruptcy
  • •Liquidation, damage collateral, scrap value, new purchase, repair consultation, and quality confirmation appraisals
  • •Gemstone identification, certificate verification, natural vs. lab-grown diamond review, and expert consulting

Appraisal Process Notes

The official appraisals page says appointments include consultation, inspection, light cleaning, professional photography, and a detailed typed description. It also states that standard appraisal fees are $125 for the first item and $75 for each additional item, with on-site or in-home services available for an added travel fee.
