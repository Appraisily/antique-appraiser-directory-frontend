Appraisal Process Notes

The official appraisals page says appointments include consultation, inspection, light cleaning, professional photography, and a detailed typed description. It also states that standard appraisal fees are $125 for the first item and $75 for each additional item, with on-site or in-home services available for an added travel fee.
