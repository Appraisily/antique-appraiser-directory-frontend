Concept Art Gallery

Pittsburgh, PA

Concept Art Gallery is a Pittsburgh gallery and auction house offering art appraisal services. Its official appraisals page says gallery directors Sam Berkovitz and Alison Brand Oehler are licensed auctioneers and certified members of the Appraisers Association of America, and that appraisal services are available by appointment by email or phone.

Specialties

  • •Fine art appraisal for Pittsburgh collectors, estates, corporations, and institutions
  • •Insurance, estate, charitable gift, and sales-purpose valuation work
  • •Modern and contemporary art, prints, graphics, antiques, jewelry, books, furniture, and design auctions

Services

  • •Art appraisal appointments by phone or email
  • •Art auctions, brokered sales, art handling, and custom framing
  • •Consulting for collectors, foundations, museums, corporations, and estates
