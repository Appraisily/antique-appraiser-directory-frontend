Fort Worth Coin Company, Inc.

Fort Worth, TX

Fort Worth Coin Company, Inc. provides coin appraisals and valuation services from its Camp Bowie Boulevard shop in Fort Worth, Texas. Its official appraisal page says the business maintains a numismatist on staff and provides formal written appraisals for customers, professional advisors, fiduciaries, and executors.

Specialties

  • •Coin collections, rare coins, and numismatic valuation
  • •Flatware and jewelry valuation services
  • •Replacement value and dealer bid-value appraisal contexts

Services

  • •Formal written appraisals for estates, insurance, trusts, conservatorships, and equitable distribution
  • •Informal offers to purchase collections at the store
  • •Physical-examination appraisals based on scarcity, authenticity, condition, and precious-metals market rates when applicable
