St. Lifer Art, Inc.

New York, New York

St. Lifer Art, Inc. is a New York-based fine-art appraisal firm led by Jane St. Lifer. Its official website confirms work with private collectors, artists' estates, and corporate collections, with a focus on nineteenth-, twentieth-, and twenty-first-century art.
