Hannon Jewelers

Baton Rouge, LA

Hannon Jewelers is a Baton Rouge fine jewelry store founded in 1980. Official materials describe in-house jewelry appraisals conducted by an American Gem Society Certified Gemologist Appraiser, with services for insurance coverage, estate planning, equitable distribution, appraisal updates, and consultation appointments.

Specialties

  • •Jewelry appraisal and gemological evaluation
  • •Insurance replacement value and estate appraisal needs
  • •Fine diamonds, color gemstones, custom jewelry, and heirloom pieces

Services

  • •Standard jewelry appraisals with cleaning, testing, research, photography, and documentation
  • •Express appraisal appointments when available
  • •Appraisal updates and consultation appointments
