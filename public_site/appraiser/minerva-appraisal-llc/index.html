Minerva Appraisal, LLC

Nashville personal property appraisal firm

Minerva Appraisal, LLC is an independent personal property appraisal firm in Nashville. Official materials describe services for estates, IRS-taxable estates, divorce, bankruptcy, liquidation, insurance coverage, IRS charitable donations, damage claims, equitable distribution, downsizing, and financial planning.

The firm states that it does not buy appraised items, offer estate sales, or provide auction services. Its owner, Kirsten Rabe Smolensky, ISA CAPP, JD, is listed as an ISA Certified Appraiser and USPAP instructor.
