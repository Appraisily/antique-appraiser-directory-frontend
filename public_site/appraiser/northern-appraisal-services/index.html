Northern Appraisal Services

Worthington and Central Ohio

Northern Appraisal Services is an independent jewelry appraisal firm serving central Ohio. The official site describes appraisals of modern and antique jewelry, watches, pearls, diamonds, gemstones, and silver, with work performed by appointment at the Worthington office or on site with a mobile gemological laboratory.
