English & Associates Estate Services

Houston, TX

English & Associates Estate Services of Houston provides personal property appraisals, estate appraisals, estate sales, estate liquidations, cataloging, and consignment support. The official site describes service for individuals, fiduciaries, attorneys, estate and financial professionals, trust companies, banks, insurance companies, and organizations across Greater Houston and beyond.

Specialties

  • -Personal property, estate, general residential contents, fine art, antique, silver, insurance, and damage-claim appraisals
  • -Residential contents, antiques, decorative arts, fine art, collectibles, silver, jewelry, furs, vehicles, household contents, and tangible personal property
  • -Patricia M. English, a Houston appraiser described by the official site as qualified, experienced, and a former International Society of Appraisers member

Services

  • -Appraisals for insurance replacement cost, damage claims, property division, estate probate, charitable donation deductions, federal estate tax, bankruptcy, legal settlements, and estate planning
  • -Written or verbal appraisal opinions with inspection, inventorying, photography, identification, descriptions, provenance, research, and valuation as appropriate to the assignment
  • -Estate sales, estate liquidations, inventorying and cataloging, consignment or brokering, and property-division assistance
