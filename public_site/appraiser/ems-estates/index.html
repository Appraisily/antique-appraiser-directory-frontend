EMS Estates Jewelry Appraisals

Portland, Oregon

EMS Estates is Elizabeth Simpson's Portland fine jewelry appraisal, evaluation, consignment, direct purchase, custom design, and repair business. The official site says Elizabeth founded EMS Estates in 2015 to provide a knowledgeable and personal approach to appraising and selling fine jewelry.

Source-Backed Services

  • •Professional jewelry appraisals with detailed measurements, grading, values, certification, and professional photographs
  • •Expert evaluations with metal and gemstone testing, condition review, commercial value, and verbal evaluation
  • •Fine jewelry consignment, direct purchase, custom design, and repair services
  • •GIA standards-based appraisals for understanding current jewelry market values

Pricing Listed by the Official Site

  • •Appraisal: $120 for the first piece and $100 for each additional piece
  • •Complex appraisal pieces may require more time and may be priced higher
  • •Evaluation: $50 for a half-hour appointment and evaluation of up to ten pieces

Specialties
