Kennedy Brothers Auctions

St. Petersburg and Tampa Bay estate, auction, liquidation and appraisal services.

Kennedy Brothers Auctions has provided estate services since 1991. The official appraisal page says the team inspects objects, measures and photographs them, researches comparable objects in the appropriate market, and prepares fee-based formal written appraisal documents or lower-fee consultations.

  • •Estate sales, auctions, clean-outs and liquidation guidance
  • •Insurance, estate planning, consultation and disposal decisions

Services

  • •Antique and estate auctions, consignments, estate sales and clean-out services
