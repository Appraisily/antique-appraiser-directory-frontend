Art Appraisals of Santa Fe

Santa Fe, NM

Art Appraisals of Santa Fe provides ISA appraisal and curatorial services for American fine art, decorative arts, antiques, and Southwest Native American arts in Santa Fe, NM. Owner Michael Ettema, ISA prepares professional appraisal reports for estate, insurance, tax, charitable contribution, equitable distribution, casualty loss, collateral, and liquidation needs.

Specialties

  • •American fine art: paintings, drawings, prints, and sculpture
  • •Decorative arts and antiques: furniture, ceramics, silver, lighting, and folk art
  • •Southwest Native American rugs, blankets, pottery, Indian jewelry, baskets, kachinas, paintings, and sculpture
  • •ISA member appraisal practice and USPAP/ISA report standards

Services

  • •Professional appraisal reports
  • •Estate planning, insurance, tax, charitable contribution, equitable distribution, casualty loss, loan collateral, and liquidation appraisals
  • •Curatorial services, identification and inventory, collection planning, refining collections, and buying or selling guidance
  • •Email-based preliminary review from photographs when appropriate
