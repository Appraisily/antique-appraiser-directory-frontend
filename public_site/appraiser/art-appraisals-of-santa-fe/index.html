Art Appraisals of Santa Fe

Santa Fe, NM

Art Appraisals of Santa Fe provides ISA appraisal and curatorial services for American fine art, decorative arts, antiques, and Southwest Native American arts in Santa Fe, NM. Owner Michael Ettema, ISA prepares professional appraisal reports for estate, insurance, tax, charitable contribution, equitable distribution, casualty loss, collateral, and liquidation needs.
