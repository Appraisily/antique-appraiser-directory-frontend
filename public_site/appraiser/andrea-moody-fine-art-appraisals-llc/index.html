Andrea Moody Fine Art Appraisals, LLC

Seattle, WA

Andrea Moody Fine Art Appraisals, LLC provides fine art appraisal, cataloging, resale, and research services for Seattle, the Pacific Northwest, and Washington, DC clients. Andrea Moody launched the practice in 1999 after receiving accreditation through the International Society of Appraisers and works with collectors, estates, insurers, and clients new to the art market.
