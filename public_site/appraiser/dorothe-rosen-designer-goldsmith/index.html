Studio Background

The official artist page says Dorothée graduated from NSCAD University in 2005 with a major in jewellery design and metalsmithing and creates jewellery in her Halifax, Nova Scotia studio. It also says she works with certified Canadian diamonds or laboratory grown diamonds, SCS certified recycled gold, Fairmined ECO Gold, and Fairmined sterling silver.
