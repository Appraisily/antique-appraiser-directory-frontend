Artem Appraisals

Providence, RI

Artem Appraisals is a Providence, Rhode Island personal property appraisal company covering fine art, antiques, furnishings, jewelry, and decorative arts. The firm lists USPAP-compliant appraisal, valuation, and consulting services for private individuals, institutions, attorneys, corporations, and insurance providers.

Specialties

  • •Fine art, antiques, furnishings, and decorative arts
  • •Jewelry, diamonds, gemstones, watches, collectibles, and household goods
  • •USPAP and International Society of Appraisers aligned appraisal practice

Services

  • •Insurance coverage, damage and loss, estate, tax, donation, gift tax, equitable distribution, and probate appraisals
  • •Formal appraisal reports based on on-site examination, documentation, research, and market analysis
  • •Consulting for heirs, trustees, downsizing, collection planning, inventory, cataloging, provenance, and purchase or sale advisement
