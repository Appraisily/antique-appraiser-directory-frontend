Kurt Shaw & Company, Inc.

Pittsburgh-area art, antiques, furniture and personal property appraiser.

Source-Backed Profile

Kurt Shaw & Company, Inc. provides Pittsburgh-area personal property appraisal services for art, antiques, furniture and collections. Public company pages describe appraisal work for insurance, estates, charitable donation, divorce, downsizing and shipping purposes.

Kurt Shaw is listed as IFAA certified, with more than 20 years of appraisal experience, plus memberships in the International Fine Art Appraisers, American Alliance of Museums and American Bar Association.
