Manzi Appraisers & Restoration

Woburn, MA | Boston by appointment | New England and broader service by inquiry

Manzi Appraisers & Restoration provides personal property appraisal reports and restoration services for art, furniture, decorative objects, antiques, and collections. The official appraisal services page says its reports follow USPAP and include physical examination, inventory, research, and narrative reporting.

Appraisal And Restoration Focus

  • -Personal property appraisals, estate tax valuations, insurance reports, and equitable distribution reports
  • -Damage/loss reports, replacement value research, and contents inventory services
  • -Fine art, antiques, furniture, decorative objects, books, maps, photographs, and mirrors
  • -Restoration, framing, upholstery, object conservation, art handling, transportation, and storage
