Emerald Art Services LLC

Akron, Ohio

Emerald Art Services LLC provides personal property consulting and appraisal services from Akron, Ohio. The official site describes reports written to ISA and USPAP standards, appraisal work for fine art, collectibles, ceramics, silver, antiques, archival materials, wine, and related personal property, plus licensed private investigation services in Ohio.

Qualifications

The official qualifications page identifies Mr. Cris Drugan, MIPAV-OS, LPQ as principal at Emerald Art Services LLC and states that he is an IRS qualified appraiser with ISA appraisal-methodology education, USPAP training, and experience in fine art claims adjusting, art consulting, restoration, loss prevention, and investigation work.
