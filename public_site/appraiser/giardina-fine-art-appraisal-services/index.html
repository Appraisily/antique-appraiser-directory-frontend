GIARDINA Fine Art Appraisal Services

PO Box 38066, Albany, NY 12203

GIARDINA Fine Art Appraisal Services provides professional fine art appraisal reports for insurance coverage, damage and loss, estates and trusts, equitable distribution, non-cash charitable contributions, acquisition, and disposition. Owner Bernadette Giardina is listed as an Accredited Senior Appraiser with the American Society of Appraisers and USPAP compliant.

Specialties

  • •Fine art appraisal reports for insurance, damage and loss, estate, trust, equitable distribution, donation, acquisition, and disposition uses
  • •Paintings, sculpture, works on paper, photography, and related personal property categories
  • •USPAP-compliant appraisal process, market analysis, cataloguing, condition review, provenance research, and report documentation

Services

  • •Insurance coverage appraisals at retail replacement value
  • •Estate tax, estate planning, probate, equitable distribution, and divorce-related fair market value reports
  • •IRS-related non-cash charitable contribution appraisals and acquisition or disposition valuation support
