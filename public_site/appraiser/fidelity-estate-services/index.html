Fidelity Estate Services

Phoenix, AZ

Fidelity Estate Services is a Phoenix-based Arizona estate liquidation company offering estate auctions, estate sales, buyouts, certified personal property appraisals, and real estate representation. Official service copy says its certified appraisals are used for insurance claims, charitable donations, divorce, bankruptcy, and liquidation, with appraisals available in Phoenix and throughout Arizona.

Specialties

  • •Certified personal property appraisals in Phoenix and throughout Arizona
  • •Antiques, collectibles, guns, fine art, estate jewelry, gold and silver coins, and rare books
  • •Native American baskets, pottery and rugs, household furniture, and decorative items
  • •ISA Accredited Member, CAGA Certified Appraiser, USPAP certified, and Qualified IRS Appraiser requirements

Services

  • •Estate auctions and estate sales for illness, death, divorce, downsizing, and assisted-living moves
  • •Written appraisal reports with examination, photography, documentation, and research
  • •Estate buyouts for entire estates, collections, and individual high-value items
  • •Estate-process coordination including personal property liquidation and real estate representation
