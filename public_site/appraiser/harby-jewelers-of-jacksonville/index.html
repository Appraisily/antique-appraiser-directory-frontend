Harby Jewelers of Jacksonville

Jacksonville, FL

Harby Jewelers of Jacksonville provides jewelry appraisals from its Riverplace Tower store in Jacksonville. The business describes two Jacksonville jewelry appraisers who are Graduate Gemologists from the Gemological Institute of America, with appraisal work available for insurance, estate, tax, resale, fair market value, and family-division needs.

Specialties

  • •Jacksonville jewelry appraisals
  • •Insurance and replacement-value appraisals
  • •Estate, tax, resale, and fair market value appraisals
  • •Family jewelry division and informal listings

Services

  • •Drop-off jewelry appraisals
  • •While-you-wait appraisal appointments
  • •Item descriptions, cleaning, photos, gemstone identification, and condition review
  • •Advisory help choosing the correct appraisal purpose

Published Appraisal Fees
