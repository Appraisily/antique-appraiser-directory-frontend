Directory Verification Updates

Antique Appraiser Directory by Appraisily · Last reviewed July 28, 2026

This page records material, factual changes to the directory’s provider verification, publication states, routing, and quality controls. It does not publish private complaints, unresolved allegations, or internal reviewer discussion.

“Verified” and “source-listed” describe the scope of evidence checked for a directory record. They are not recommendations, employment relationships, guarantees of availability, or substitutes for confirming assignment fit directly. Read the directory methodology for the complete definitions.

How to read this log: a profile update records only the claim scope that passed its evidence gate. A city update does not establish that every business shown serves that city unless the provider card and profile say so explicitly.
July 28, 2026

Limited-profile claim boundaries made explicit

The directory applied a blocking claim-scope contract to all 239 source-listed profiles. A record approved only for business identity and official website can no longer present location, service area, specialties, credentials, assignment types, fees, turnaround, or availability as verified facts.

All 239 source-listed profiles now use the complete visible limited-state contract. Their first-party directory illustrations also state when location is not verified, and immutable artwork references are versioned so stale location labels cannot survive a source release in browser cache.

July 28, 2026

City publication and usefulness gates tightened

Indexable city pages must now have a crawlable provider profile plus reviewed provider evidence or city-specific decision support. Visible provider cards must match the provider identities and routes in structured data.

Miami was changed to noindex, follow and removed from the sitemap because it did not yet have a provider that cleared the publication gate. Other ranking city records remain under page-by-page review; unsupported provider cards and claims are not converted into verified listings automatically, and the frozen GSC cohort must not be bulk-noindexed.

July 27, 2026

Art-directory records consolidated

The separate Art Appraisers Directory was retired as an indexable property. Five reviewed provider profiles moved into this directory, and approved legacy routes were mapped to their consolidated destinations. Unresolved provider identities remain terminal rather than being recreated from old thin pages.

The consolidation preserves one canonical directory for local appraisal discovery while keeping Appraisily’s online appraisal service separate.

What appears in future updates

  • Provider publication-state changes supported by a reviewed source.
  • Material corrections to identity, canonical URL, location, credential, or service claims.
  • City indexability or provider-discovery changes.
  • Changes to the evidence, schema, routing, or anti-duplication contracts.

We do not publish correction-requester identities, private correspondence, complaint narratives, or unresolved accusations. A correction is reflected here only when it changes a public directory fact or policy.
