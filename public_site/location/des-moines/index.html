Iowa historical-material decisions

Separate preservation, identification, donation, and monetary appraisal before you request a quote

The State Historical Society of Iowa publishes collection-care guidance for objects, papers, photographs, and records, but states that it does not provide authentication or monetary appraisal. For a Des Moines-area family collection, first identify the decision you need: safer storage, object identification, donation review, insurance documentation, estate value, or resale advice.

Document before handling

Photograph the object and its current storage, record dimensions, condition, labels, inscriptions, repairs, and ownership history, and keep related papers together. Use the printable preparation checklist.

Define the assignment and site

Give the exact inspection address, building and storage access, number and scale of objects, intended recipient, value basis, effective date, and required report. Do not assume a business found elsewhere in Iowa serves Des Moines.
