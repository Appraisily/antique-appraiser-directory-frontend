Plan a Kelowna or Okanagan appraisal before moving the property

Kelowna owners may be comparing a local visit with travel from another British Columbia market. For a large painting, furniture piece, estate grouping, or fragile object, ask for the inspection location, travel boundary, transport expectations, and site-visit charges before moving anything. Photos can help a professional decide whether remote triage is sufficient, but they do not prove that a remote inspection will satisfy an insurer, estate, donor, court, or buyer.

Send an inventory with dimensions, maker marks, condition details, ownership history, and wide and close photographs. State the assignment purpose at the beginning: resale advice, an auction estimate, an insurance replacement-value report, estate administration, donation support, and equitable distribution can require different value definitions, effective dates, research, independence, and signed documentation.

Questions to resolve before a Kelowna assignment

  • Does the provider currently serve Kelowna or the wider Okanagan, and is the inspection remote, at a business location, or on site?
  • For furniture or a multi-item estate, what travel, transport, photography, and access requirements apply before the assignment begins?
  • Is the professional providing an independent written appraisal, or only an auction estimate, purchase offer, or consignment opinion?
  • Will the report use the value definition, effective date, research, and documentation required by the insurer, estate, donor, court, or buyer?
