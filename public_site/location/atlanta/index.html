Atlanta collection readiness

Plan the inspection around access, evacuation readiness, and the report’s actual recipient

Atlanta’s Office of Emergency Preparedness advises residents to make a plan, assemble essentials, and stay informed through local alerts that can include severe weather, road closures, and evacuations. Apply the same discipline to valuable property before an appraisal: know where every item is stored, who can authorize access, what can be moved safely, and which records must remain available if a building or storage location becomes inaccessible.

Create an access and condition record

Photograph the full object, details, marks, damage, frame or mount, and current room or storage position. Record dimensions, floor, elevator or loading constraints, ownership documents, and the last known undamaged condition. Use the printable preparation checklist before moving anything.

Match the assignment before scheduling

Tell the appraiser whether the work is for insurance, estate administration, donation, equitable distribution, resale, or identification. Confirm the intended recipient, value basis, effective date, signer requirements, inspection mode, number of locations, and whether severe-weather or building-access changes require a remote first pass.
