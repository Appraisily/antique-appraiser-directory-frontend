Flood, storm, and custody preparation

Record condition and intended value before a New Orleans object is moved or repaired

NOLA Ready treats flooding, evacuation, insurance records, and protection of belongings as current household-planning issues. For art, furniture, papers, or collections exposed to water, humidity, outages, or storm handling, tell the appraiser what happened and when instead of presenting only the object’s current appearance.

Preserve the evidence trail

Keep pre-event photographs, inventories, receipts, prior reports, repair records, insurance correspondence, current condition images, and the dates of any move, water exposure, drying, cleaning, or restoration.

Confirm scope before transport

Ask whether the assignment concerns replacement value, fair market value, damage or loss, estate use, or resale; give the effective date, exact floor and access conditions, object dimensions, and custody plan. Confirm whether photographs are sufficient before moving a vulnerable or oversized item.
