Los Angeles inspection and hazard planning

Choose the safest evidence path before earthquakes, fire, heat, or building access affect the assignment

The City of Los Angeles identifies earthquakes, wildfires, flooding, mudslides, and extreme heat among local threats. For a collection spread across a house, high-rise, studio, gallery, or storage facility, the appraisal plan should therefore begin with exact locations, safe access, object stability, and a documented alternative if travel or entry changes. This is assignment planning, not a claim that any listed business covers the whole region.

Triage without creating new damage

Photograph each item in place before lifting it. Record dimensions, weight, hanging or mounting method, existing cracks, loose components, smoke or water exposure, frame and glazing, storage climate, stairs, elevators, parking, loading access, and any packer or conservator already involved. Do not clean a damaged surface for the photograph.

Confirm the value question and recipient

Insurance, estate, donation, litigation, resale, and identification assignments can require different value bases, effective dates, inspection scopes, signers, and report formats. Ask the recipient first, then organize provenance, earlier reports, inventory numbers, and images with the preparation checklist.
