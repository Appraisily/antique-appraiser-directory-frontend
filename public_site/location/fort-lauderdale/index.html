Plan a Fort Lauderdale appraisal around purpose, access, and conflicts

A Fort Lauderdale assignment may involve a condo, waterfront residence, storage unit, business inventory, or an estate spread across multiple locations. Before booking, identify which property must be inspected, who can provide access, whether travel or handling fees apply, and whether fragile or storm-displaced objects should remain in place until the professional gives instructions.

State whether you need resale guidance, an auction estimate, insurance replacement value, estate administration, donation support, litigation, or equitable distribution. Ask who will sign the report, which value definition and effective date it will use, and whether the provider or a related party may also buy, auction, consign, liquidate, store, or transport the property.
