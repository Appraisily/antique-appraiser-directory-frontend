How to choose an appraiser in Kansas

Hiring the right antique and art appraisers in Wichita, Kansas depends on your goal (insurance, estate settlement, donation, or resale). Look for clear fees, a written report when needed, and evidence of comparable research for your item type.

Before you contact a provider, gather photos, measurements, and any provenance. Ask about turnaround time, what kind of value the report uses (fair market vs replacement), and whether the fee includes a written report.

Use the assignment to choose the next step

For insurance, ask whether the report states the policy’s required value basis. For an estate or donation, confirm the effective date, intended use, signer qualifications, and documentation requirements with the relevant professional before ordering. For resale curiosity, a remote first look may be enough until the item’s likely significance justifies inspection.

Prepare before contacting a provider

Photograph the full item, maker marks, damage, repairs, construction details, and scale. Gather measurements, receipts, prior reports, provenance, and the date or purpose for which value is needed. These details help a provider decide whether remote review is sufficient or an in-person examination is necessary.
