Cleveland winter-access planning

Document the collection before snow, ice, outages, or parking restrictions change the inspection plan

Cleveland’s preparedness guidance calls for portable essential supplies and important documents, while its winter guidance warns that snow and ice can create power outages, hazardous driving, and temporary parking restrictions. For an appraisal, plan for the same interruptions: record the objects and storage locations first, confirm whether a safe loading route will be available, and preserve an evidence copy that does not depend on reaching the property.

Control the temperature and handling transition

Photograph each item in place, including marks, labels, mounts, bases, cracks, loose parts, corrosion, staining, and storage context. Record dimensions, weight, floor, stairs, elevator, parking and loading access, and whether an unheated vehicle or abrupt indoor/outdoor temperature change would affect safe movement. Do not clean for the appointment.

Make the report request usable off site

Keep provenance, receipts, prior appraisals, insurance schedules, and an item-by-item image inventory in a secure second location. Give the appraiser the intended recipient, use, value basis, effective date, signer requirements, deadline, and inspection constraints using the preparation checklist.
