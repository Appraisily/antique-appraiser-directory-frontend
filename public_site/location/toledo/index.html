Toledo incident and transport planning

Prepare separate evidence for storms, flooding, hazardous exposure, and ordinary appraisal handling

The joint Lucas County–City of Toledo emergency plan addresses severe thunderstorms and tornadoes, flooding, earthquakes, hazardous-material releases, evacuation, debris, and recovery. That range matters when documenting antiques and art: condition caused by an incident must be distinguishable from age, wear, an old repair, or damage introduced during a rushed move. Record the item and its surroundings before any transport or cleanup.

Preserve incident context

Photograph the full object, marks, labels, frame or base, existing repairs, water line, soot, debris, staining, distortion, detached material, packing, and storage position. Note the date and known cause of a change. Prioritize personal safety and professional conservation guidance; do not clean an unknown residue or discard damaged components for a clearer photograph.

Write the assignment boundary

Clarify whether the question is insurance, estate, donation, family division, resale, identification, pre-loss value, or current condition. Confirm the recipient, value basis, effective date, inspection scope, signer requirements, report format, and exact access using the preparation checklist.
