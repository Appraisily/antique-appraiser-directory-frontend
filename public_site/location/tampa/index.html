How to evaluate a Tampa appraisal option

Start with the intended use: informal identification, resale planning, insurance, estate administration, charitable contribution, litigation, or another documented purpose can require different value definitions, inspection depth, research, signer qualifications, and report formats.

Before contacting anyone, gather overall and detail photographs, marks, measurements, condition notes, prior repairs, receipts, and provenance. Use the printable preparation checklist, then ask the intended recipient what documentation it will accept.

Questions about location and inspection

  • Does the named professional currently serve the exact Tampa-area address, and is the work performed in person, remotely, or through a documented hybrid review?
  • Who handles custody, transport, unpacking, and access when furniture, framed art, a collection, or storage contents cannot be moved safely?
  • Are travel, parking, gated access, stairs, elevators, multiple rooms, off-site storage, or a second inspection included in the quoted scope?

Questions about independence and the report

  • Does the professional have current qualifications and object-category experience appropriate to this assignment?
  • What value definition, effective date, comparable evidence, limiting conditions, photographs, signature, and certification will the report contain?
  • Does the provider also buy, sell, consign, auction, or liquidate property, and how will any financial interest or referral relationship be disclosed?
