How to evaluate a Denver appraisal option

State the intended use first: insurance, estate administration, equitable distribution, charitable contribution, litigation, resale planning, and identification can require different value definitions, effective dates, research, signer qualifications, and report formats.

Use the printable preparation checklist to collect photographs, marks, measurements, condition notes, repairs, receipts, provenance, storage location, access instructions, and custody contacts before requesting scope or price.

Questions about inspection and travel

  • Which exact address will be inspected, and does the quoted travel boundary include the relevant municipality, foothill, or mountain location?
  • Will the named signer personally inspect, and are parking, loading access, stairs, elevators, storage, road conditions, or multiple stops included?
  • Who handles custody, packing, transport, climate transitions, and condition documentation when property must move?

Questions about independence and the report

  • What current qualifications and object-category experience does the named signer bring to this assignment?
  • What value definition, effective date, comparable evidence, assumptions, photographs, signature, and certification will appear?
  • Does the provider buy, sell, consign, auction, insure, move, or liquidate property, and how are financial interests or referrals disclosed?
