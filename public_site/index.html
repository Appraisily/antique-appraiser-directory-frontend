Know what the directory has verified

A verified profile has a dated manual review against the stated official sources and lists the exact claim scope reviewed. A source-listed profile has more limited evidence and must not be read as credential verification, endorsement, or employment by Appraisily.

Choose by assignment

Start with the decision you need to make

  • Fine art and paintings when artist, medium, provenance, or an in-person inspection matters.
  • Antiques and personal property for furniture, silver, ceramics, tools, collectibles, and estates.
  • Insurance documentation when the required value basis and report scope come from a policy or insurer.
  • Donation support when qualification, signer, timing, and tax-documentation requirements must be confirmed before engagement.

When local inspection matters

Choose a local provider when the assignment, client, insurer, court, tax adviser, or appraiser requires physical inspection, or when construction, condition, marks, scale, or authenticity cannot be documented reliably from photographs. An online appraisal can be useful when the assignment permits remote evidence and the item can be documented clearly.
