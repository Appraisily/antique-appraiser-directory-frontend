What to compare before you book

  • For art appraisal work, compare medium expertise, comparable-sales depth, written-report scope, and whether the provider supports insurance, estate, donation, or resale needs.
  • Prepare clear photos, dimensions, provenance, and condition notes before contacting anyone.
  • Use online intake first if you want a faster first-pass valuation before deciding on in-person work.
